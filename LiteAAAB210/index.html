LiteAAA on a B210

Status: step 1 of 5 works. The rest is still a plan.

What we have: a SIM card, a phone, a B210 and a PC. No shield box and no radio licence.

Steps

  1. Done, 12 November 2026. The B210 sends a Bluetooth packet and the phone sees it. In 22 seconds the phone heard 208 packets named "LiteAAA" from the B210, and none with the B210 silent.
  2. The B210 hears a Bluetooth packet sent by the phone.
  3. Wrap those two as the Reach plug-in.
  4. Phone app: the device half, with the real SIM card as the SIM plug-in.
  5. Run it: page, the SIM verifies the network, the network verifies the SIM, nonce.

Open questions

  • A Bluetooth packet carries 31 bytes. The challenge is 32, so it goes in two packets.
  • A phone does not listen all the time, so a page may have to be repeated.
  • A real SIM card checks the sequence number more strictly than our software one.
  • The app needs special access to the SIM card. The test phone is rooted, and this was done before on it.
