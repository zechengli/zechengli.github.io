LiteAAA on a B210

Status: it works over a real cellular link (LTE). 12 November 2026.

What we have: a SIM card, a OnePlus 13, a B210 and a PC. The OnePlus 13's own modem attaches to our cell over the air, and then LiteAAA runs over that cellular connection — with the real SIM giving the proof.

Structure

What happens

  1. The B210 and the PC run a small 4G (LTE) cell. The OnePlus 13's own modem attaches to it over the air and authenticates with the real SIM card — the standard mutual authentication every phone does. This gives it a data link.
  2. Over that data link, the LiteAAA app runs its own paging, challenge and nonce — our protocol, asking the same real SIM.

We tried 5G standalone as well. The OnePlus 13 will only find 5G as an add-on to 4G, so it never camps a standalone 5G cell; 4G works.
