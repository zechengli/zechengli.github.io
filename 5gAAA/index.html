5G paging demo

Paging is how a mobile network reaches a phone that is asleep. This demo runs a real 5G core (Open5GS) with a simulated cell and three simulated phones (UERANSIM): Alice, Bob and Carol.

Looking for the demo on this computer ...

To use the buttons, open this page on the lab PC and run cd /data2/work/paging-demo && ./paging up there. If the browser asks to allow access to devices on your local network, choose Allow. Without that, the table below shows a recorded run.

The phones

CONNECTED: the phone has a radio link. IDLE: the phone is asleep and can only be reached by paging. LOST: the phone is out of coverage and the network does not know it.

The paging occasion is calculated from the phone's identity (3GPP TS 38.304 section 7.1, with T=128, N=64, Ns=2). The simulated radio delivers a page at once and does not wait for that moment.

How paging works

  1. Why. A phone that kept its radio link up all day would drain its battery and waste the cell's capacity. After a quiet period the network releases the link and the phone goes idle. It keeps its registration and its IP address.
  2. What starts it. Data arrives for an idle phone. The core cannot deliver it, so it holds the packet and asks for the phone to be paged.
  3. Where. The network only remembers which tracking area an idle phone is in, so the page goes to every cell in that area.
  4. When. An idle phone wakes up briefly once per cycle, at a moment worked out from its own identity. Phones with different identities wake at different moments.
  5. Who answers. The page carries one phone's temporary identity (5G-TMSI). Only that phone answers, with a Service Request. The connection is rebuilt and the held packet is delivered. The phone then gets a new temporary identity.
  6. No answer. If nobody answers within 2 seconds the network pages again. After the third try it gives up and drops the packet.

Things to try

  1. Press "Set all phones idle".
  2. Press "Call" for Alice. Only Alice becomes CONNECTED, and her 5G-TMSI changes.
  3. Press "Use" for Carol. She wakes up by herself, with no page.
  4. Set all phones idle, press "Lose" for Bob, then "Call" for Bob. The network pages three times and gives up. This takes about 10 seconds.
  5. Press "Find" for Bob, then "Call" for Bob again.
