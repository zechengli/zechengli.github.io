5gAAA · live demo

Paging + 5G AKA instead of an MFA push and challenge

The network wakes a sleeping phone by paging it (the push notification), then challenges its SIM with a fresh 5G AKA: RAND and AUTN go out, RES* comes back.

The buttons below drive the real lab demo (Open5GS + UERANSIM) one radio message at a time.
